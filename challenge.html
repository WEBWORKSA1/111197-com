Wish Grant FAQ

Do I need to donate or buy anything?

No. Entry is free and donations have no effect on judging.

Who can enter?

Adults 18+ where the contest is not prohibited by law. Employees of 111197 and sponsors’ judging staff are excluded. Full eligibility is in the Official Rules.

How is the prize paid?

By bank transfer or a reputable payment provider after identity and eligibility checks. We never ask for card numbers or passwords by email or form.

Can I enter more than once?

One entry per person per cycle. Non-winning entries may be re-submitted in later cycles.
