Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Summary

Calculator and planner inputs stay in your browser. We only receive personal information when you choose to submit a form. We do not sell personal information. Google may use cookies to serve ads, as described below.

Information we collect

  • Forms you submit — e.g. name, email, optional phone, goal details, contest entries, applications, donation pledges and messages.
  • Technical data — standard server and analytics data such as browser type, pages viewed and approximate location, collected by our hosting and advertising providers.
  • Local storage — your theme, currency and cookie choice, saved on your device only.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and deliver the plan, newsletter or service you asked for.
  • To run the Wish Grant (eligibility, judging, prize payment).
  • To make an introduction to one partner — only if you explicitly opt in.
  • To keep the site secure, measure performance and show advertising.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), a certified consent management message asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

Service providers

Forms are delivered through a third-party form-processing service; the site is hosted on GitHub Pages; videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only after you press play. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the site, with one matched partner when you request an introduction, when required by law, or to protect rights and safety. We never sell or rent lists.

Retention & security

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you gave them (typically up to 24 months) and protect them with reasonable safeguards. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, Québec Law 25, CCPA/CPRA, India’s DPDP Act), you may request access, correction, deletion or portability of your data, withdraw consent, or object to processing. Send requests via the contact page (topic: Privacy request). We respond within 30 days.

Children

The site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or 16 where required). Contest entry requires age 18+.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
