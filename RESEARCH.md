## 2. Concept chosen: **111197 — 11:11 Plan Lab**
"Stop wishing at 11:11. Start a plan that pays." A free **Wish → Plan** platform: turns any wish into a dated, 11-step money plan, plus calculators and plain-English guides for Gen Z and first-jobbers.

**Why this wins**
- Bridges a massive, free traffic theme (11:11 / angel numbers / 11.11) to **personal finance — the highest-RPM AdSense niche** (finance/insurance RPM commonly cited at $15–$50 in tier-1 markets).
- Tools = repeat visits, long sessions, natural ad inventory, backlinks.
- Ownable framework ("The 11 Steps", "$1,111 starter cushion", "debt above 11%", "11th Review", "$11.11 a day") makes the number itself the brand's habit cue.
- Lead gen is natural: people with a goal + number + date are high-intent for coaches, credit unions, counsellors, career coaches.

## 3. Revenue model
1. **Google AdSense** (auto ads; finance RPM) — main passive stream.
2. **Lead generation**: 5-step "free personal plan" funnel with opt-in partner match → per-lead / retainer fees from coaches, credit unions, counselling, career coaches, SMB advisors.
3. **Sponsorships**: tool "presented by", Wish Grant cycle, 11th Review newsletter, 11/11 Season takeover.
4. **Donations**: one-time + monthly circles ($11.11 / $19.97 / $111.97), directed to operations, prizes, promotion, hiring.
5. **Contest**: 11:11 Wish Grant (monthly, skill-based, free entry) → UGC, email list, sponsor inventory.
6. **Domain/website sale & partnerships**: banner on every page → web.works/contact.
