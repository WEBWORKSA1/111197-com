FAQ

Questions people ask

Is 111197 a financial advisor?

No. 111197 is an educational site with free planning tools. We don’t sell investments or give individual advice. For personal advice, speak with a licensed professional in your country — the free plan form can request an introduction.

Do you store the numbers I type into the calculators?

No. Calculators and the Wish → Plan generator run entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to submit a form.

Does 11:11 actually mean something?

For many people it’s a cultural or spiritual moment; scientifically, noticing it is explained by attention and pattern-seeking. We treat it as a useful reminder — and a reminder is only as good as the action that follows. Read the full story.

How is the site funded?

Through advertising (Google AdSense), sponsorships, partner introductions you request, and reader support. Editorial content is independent — see our editorial policy.
