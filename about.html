About

We turn “someday” into a date.

111197 — read “1111 97” — is a free planning lab for people who are done wishing and ready to start. It’s built first for Gen Z (the generation that begins in 1997) and anyone earning their first real paychecks, starting over, or finally tackling a goal they’ve postponed.

Why we exist

Money advice is everywhere; money plans are rare. Most people know they should save, pay down debt or earn more. What’s missing is a specific amount, a date and the next step — written down. Our tools do exactly that, in minutes, for free, without collecting the numbers you type.

What we believe

  • Specific beats inspired. A wish becomes a goal when it has a number and a date.
  • Order matters. Cushion first, expensive debt next, then acceleration — that’s the 11-step framework.
  • Small and automatic wins. $11.11 a day changes lives because it happens every day.
  • Honesty over hype. We label beliefs as beliefs, estimates as estimates and ads as ads.

How we make money

We keep the tools free through display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, referral fees from partners readers choose to be introduced to, and reader support. None of these can buy a recommendation or change a calculator result. Read the editorial policy.
