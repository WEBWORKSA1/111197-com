Editorial policy

Last updated: November 2026

Our standard

Every page should help a reader take a correct next step. We write in plain English, show our math, cite primary sources (government agencies, central banks, peer-reviewed research and established surveys), and label opinions, beliefs and estimates as such.

Research & review

  • Guides are drafted by a writer, then fact-checked by a second person before publication.
  • Money guides are scheduled for review at least once a year, and sooner when rules or rates change.
  • Statistics include the source and year; we prefer the most recent primary data.
  • AI tools may assist with research and drafting; a human editor is accountable for every published word.

Calculators

Calculators use standard formulas (future value of a series, amortization, percentage rules) and run in your browser. Assumptions are shown next to each tool. Results are estimates, not offers or advice.

Spiritual & cultural topics

Where we discuss 11:11, angel numbers or numerology, we describe them as cultural beliefs and explain the psychology alongside them. We never claim a number predicts outcomes.

Advertising & independence

We earn money from Google AdSense display ads, labelled sponsorships, partner referral fees for introductions readers request, and reader support. Advertisers and partners do not see or edit content before publication and cannot change calculator results or rankings. Sponsored placements are labelled “Sponsored” or “Presented by.”

Corrections

Spotted an error? Use the contact form with topic “Correction.” We review every report and update the page with a dated note when a material correction is made.
