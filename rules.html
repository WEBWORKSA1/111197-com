11:11 Wish Grant — Official Rules

Last updated: November 2026

NO PURCHASE, DONATION OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A purchase or donation will not increase your chances of winning. This is a skill-based contest judged on the criteria below; it is not a lottery or sweepstakes.

1. Organizer

The 11:11 Wish Grant (the “Contest”) is organized by the operator of 111197.com (the “Organizer”). Questions may be sent through the contact page.

2. Eligibility

Open to individuals who are 18 or older (or the age of majority in their place of residence, if higher) at the time of entry. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Employees and contractors of the Organizer, judges, and members of their immediate households are not eligible. Residents of jurisdictions that require registration or bonding of skill contests are eligible only where the Organizer has completed those requirements; residents of Québec should note that the Organizer may restrict eligibility in cycles where Régie requirements apply.

3. Contest period & how to enter

Each monthly cycle opens immediately after the previous cycle closes and closes on the 11th day of the month at 11:11 a.m. U.S. Eastern Time. Enter by completing the form on the Wish Grant page with a goal, target amount, target date and written plan (maximum 1,111 characters). Limit one entry per person per cycle. Entries must be original, the entrant’s own work, and must not include hateful, sexual, unlawful or infringing content, or another person’s personal information.

4. Prizes

The prize for each cycle (the “Cycle Prize”) is stated on the Wish Grant page before that cycle opens and is funded by reader support and sponsors. The Season 1 target prize is US$1,111 per cycle. If a cycle’s prize has not been confirmed on the page when the cycle opens, that cycle runs as a recognition cycle and winners receive a site feature only. Prizes are paid in the stated currency by bank transfer or a reputable payment provider within 30 days of verification, are non-transferable, and no substitution is allowed except at the Organizer’s discretion for a prize of equal or greater value. Winners are responsible for any taxes.

5. Judging

A panel appointed by the Organizer scores eligible entries on: clarity (30%), realism (30%), impact (25%) and creativity (15%). In a tie, the entry with the higher realism score wins. Canadian residents selected as potential winners must correctly answer a time-limited mathematical skill-testing question before being confirmed. Judges’ decisions are final.

6. Winner notification

Potential winners are notified by email within 11 days of the cycle closing and must respond within 7 days and complete an eligibility declaration. Failure to respond or comply may result in forfeiture and selection of an alternate winner.

7. Publicity & licence

By entering you grant the Organizer a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display your first name, country and a summary of your plan on 111197.com and related channels if you opted in or are selected as a winner. You keep ownership of your entry.

8. Privacy

Personal information is used to run the Contest and as described in our Privacy Policy. We will never ask for card numbers, passwords or government ID numbers by email or form.

9. General conditions

The Organizer may cancel, suspend or modify the Contest if fraud, technical failure or any other factor impairs its integrity, and may disqualify any entrant who tampers with the entry process. The Organizer is not responsible for lost, late or incomplete entries. This Contest is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Google, YouTube, any social network or any “11.11” shopping festival.

10. Governing law

These rules are governed by the laws applicable at the Organizer’s place of business, without regard to conflict-of-law principles, except where local consumer law grants you additional rights.
